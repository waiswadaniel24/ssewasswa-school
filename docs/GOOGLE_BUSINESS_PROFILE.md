This is the **only "free advertising on Google" that actually exists**. Google Business Profile (GBP) is free for any business with a physical location or service area. Once verified, your business shows up on:

- **Google Maps** when people search "school ERP Uganda", "hospital management Uganda", "software company Kampala", etc.
- **Google Search** as a "knowledge panel" on the right side when people search your business name
