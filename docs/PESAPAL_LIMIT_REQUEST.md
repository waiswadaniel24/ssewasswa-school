# Pesapal Transaction Limit Increase — Email Request

## Why you need this

Currently, Pesapal rejects any payment above ~UGX 100,000 with the error
`amount_exceeds_default_limit`. This blocks your Premium tier subscriptions
(School ERP Premium = UGX 300,000, HMIS Premium = UGX 400,000-1,200,000).

To unlock all tiers, you must email Pesapal support requesting a transaction
limit increase. This is a one-time setup — once approved, all future
payments of any amount will work.

---

## Step 1: Find your Pesapal account details

Log into https://pay.pesapal.com → Profile. Note down:
- Business name: **Ssewasswa Comforts Technologies**
- Account email: the email you registered with
- Merchant code / Account ID (e.g. P3XXXXX — found on the dashboard top-right)

---

---

## Step 3: Attach the following documents

1. **URSB business registration certificate** (Certificate of Incorporation)
2. **Trading license** from your local council / KCCA
3. **National ID / Passport** of the director (you)
4. **Sample invoice** (create a simple Word doc with your logo, a sample
   subscription charge, and bank details — see template below)

If you don't have all the documents, send what you have. Pesapal support is
usually responsive and will guide you on what's missing.

---

## Step 4: Follow up

- **48 hours** after sending the email, if no reply: call Pesapal Uganda
  office at **+256 414 446 778** or **+256 700 000 123** (mobile)
- Mention your merchant ID + the email subject
- Ask for the verification team / risk team
- They typically respond within 3-5 business days

---

## Step 5: After approval

- Log into your Pesapal dashboard → Settings → Transaction Limits
- Confirm the new limit (should show UGX 1,500,000 or higher)
- Test by making a UGX 300,000 payment from your own card to your business

## What happens while you wait

The good news: payments below UGX 100,000 still work — so your
**Ordinary tier** (School ERP UGX 150k, HMIS UGX 200k) MIGHT work
if Pesapal's limit is per-transaction rather than monthly. If it
still blocks, customers can pay via:
- Direct MTN MoMo / Airtel Money transfer to your numbers
- Send money + WhatsApp us the payment confirmation + we issue license manually

Once Pesapal approves the limit increase, ALL amounts work automatically
via the website checkout flow.
