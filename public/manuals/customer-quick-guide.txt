School Management System — Customer Quick Guide

WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES
• Student admissions, profiles, history, promotion and requirements
• Attendance, timetable, academics, exams and report cards
• Fees, payments, debts, accounting and purchase authorization
• Staff, payroll, deductions and qualifications
• Library, transport, gate book, broadcasting and meetings
• EMIS-ready reports, KPI dashboards, validation and data export
• Document generation, signatures, ID cards and school branding

GET STARTED
1. Open the web app or install the Windows desktop app.
2. Sign in with your school account.
3. Complete School Setup and confirm your school level.
4. Register classes and students before recording payments or results.
5. Use Settings to configure branding, users, backups and permissions.
